For access, correction, deletion, or export assistance, email the same support address with the subject "Archív privacy request". See our Privacy Policy for details. We may need to verify that a request comes from the account owner.

Community photo removal

Photographers and authorized rights holders may request the removal of an approved community photograph. Include the LEGO set number or a link to the photo so we can identify it. See our Community Photo Terms.
