Privacy Policy

Archív · Last updated October 31, 2026

Archív is an independently operated app for browsing LEGO® set information, tracking sets you own, maintaining a wishlist, and following links to other sellers. This policy covers the Archív website and its companion Android and iOS test applications. Archív is not affiliated with or endorsed by the LEGO Group. We are responsible for our own data practices, not LEGO's.

1. Information we collect

Without an account: Visitors can browse the catalog without signing in. Web hosting, requests for images or catalogs, and external sites may receive technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request details. Archív does not require your name or email to browse.

With a Google account: Firebase Authentication gives Archív your unique account identifier, name, email address, and available profile photo. We do not receive your Google password. We store your owned and wishlisted LEGO set identifiers and related details, including selection timestamps, in Cloud Firestore.

Account and usage reporting: Archív also stores account profile details and first/recent sign-in dates, counts of owned and wishlisted sets, owned themes, and activity events such as an owned/wishlist change (set number, theme, action, date and time). For signed-in users, clicks on eBay shopping links may be recorded with the account identifier and time. These events are not proof of a purchase.

Local browser or device storage: Firebase sign-in uses device or browser storage to keep you authenticated. For selected set inventories, Archív may save part-checklist progress locally on your device. This checklist is separate from the cloud-saved Owned and Wishlist selections, and may be lost when browser/app data is cleared.

2. How we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, save and synchronize owned and wishlisted sets, display collection status, operate and secure the service, provide support, and maintain private activity and collection reporting. Your saved collection and activity are not published as a public profile.

Authorized Archív administrators can access account profiles, collection summaries, and activity reports to operate the service. Other collectors do not receive access to your private records. Hosting and cloud-service providers may process technical and account information to provide the service.

3. Hosting, images and third parties

GitHub Pages hosts the public website. Google Firebase provides authentication and Cloud Firestore storage. LEGO set information is drawn from Rebrickable datasets, and product images and source references may be loaded from Rebrickable or other image hosts. These hosts can receive technical request information when content loads.

Archív may link to eBay through the eBay Partner Network and receive a commission if a qualifying purchase occurs. When you visit eBay or another third-party site, that site may set cookies, process your information, and apply its own terms and privacy policy. Archív does not receive your payment card information or verify your marketplace purchases.

4. Cookies, analytics and affiliate tracking

Archív uses Firebase authentication storage and local checklist storage as described above. Signed-in affiliate-link click events are stored in Cloud Firestore for administrative reporting. The current published Archív website does not intentionally load a standalone Impact advertising/affiliate analytics script. It does not use Firebase Analytics. Third-party destinations and embedded external images may independently use cookies or similar technologies subject to their own policies. You can control cookies and local storage through your browser and device settings, but clearing sign-in storage may log you out.

5. Account and data retention

We generally retain your account details, collection selections, and associated activity as long as your account is active or they are needed to operate the service. There is not yet a self-service Delete Account control in the current LEGO interface. To request access, correction, export, or deletion of your Archív account and associated records, email getshelfiecollect@gmail.com. We will verify that the request comes from the account owner before providing or deleting account information.

Deleting Archív records does not delete your Google account or third-party eBay account. Providers may retain certain technical logs or backups under their own policies or where retention is legally required. Please contact us if you need help exercising privacy rights applicable to your location.

6. Children

Archív is a general collection-management service and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to create accounts for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 provided personal information, contact us so we can review and address the information.

7. Security and changes

We use Firebase authentication and database access controls to restrict access to account collections and reporting. No internet-based service can guarantee perfect security. We may update this policy if the app, service providers, or information practices change, and will revise the updated date when we do.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact getshelfiecollect@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or verification codes. For use of the Service, see our Terms of Use and Contact page.
