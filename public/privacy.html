Your things. Your privacy.

Privacy policy · Updated October 4, 2026

This policy describes the standalone Archív app, where you sign in with Google and save your collection to your account.

What we store

Google sign-in supplies an account identifier, name, email address, and, when available, a profile photo. Firebase Authentication manages your sign-in. Archív does not receive your Google password.

Your saved collection can include item titles, types, descriptions, source links, images, condition, grades, quantities, purchase prices, estimated values, notes, favorites, groups, and record timestamps. Photos you upload are resized and stored with your collection records in Cloud Firestore.

Why we use it

We use this information to identify your account, show your collection, and keep it synchronized across devices. Your shelves are not published as a public profile. Access rules restrict collection access to the signed-in owner; the app operator and infrastructure providers may access data when administering, supporting, or securing the service.

Services that process information

Google Firebase provides authentication and cloud storage for collection records. GitHub Pages hosts the app. Search queries may be sent to the selected catalog provider, and loading external images or following source links contacts those services. Vinyl search passes through the app's Cloudflare Worker to Discogs. These services receive connection information such as an IP address and handle it under their own policies.

The current app does not integrate Firebase Analytics or advertising trackers. Your browser retains authentication information so you can stay signed in. Sign out on shared devices.

Catalog proposals are public

Saving an item to your shelf is private to your account. If you separately choose to propose an item to the shared catalog, Archív opens a prefilled GitHub issue for you to review and submit. Submitted issues and any attachments are public. Do not include private notes or personal information. Removing your Archív account does not remove separately submitted GitHub issues.

Take a copy with you

Open Your profile and choose Export your Archív to download your saved items, groups, notes, and uploaded photos as a JSON file. Catalog images remain external links. The download is prepared in your browser from your cloud records. Keep it somewhere private, especially if your notes or purchase details are sensitive.

Keeping and deleting your data

Collection records remain while you use your account, until you remove them or delete your account. To delete your account, open Your profile, select Delete account, type DELETE, and confirm your identity with Google. Archív removes your items, groups, notes, and uploaded photos before deleting your Firebase sign-in account. This does not delete your Google account.

If deletion is interrupted, changes to that account are blocked and you can sign in again to finish deletion. A minimal deletion marker associated with the old random account identifier remains to prevent stale sessions from recreating records. It contains no name, email, or collection content. Infrastructure providers may retain operational logs or backups under their own retention practices.

Questions or requests

Contact getshelfiecollect@gmail.com with privacy questions or if you need help accessing or deleting your information. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change.
