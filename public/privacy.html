Privacy Policy

Archív · Last updated October 31, 2026

Archív is an independently operated app for browsing LEGO® set information, tracking sets you own, maintaining a wishlist, and following links to other sellers. This policy covers the Archív website and its companion Android and iOS test applications. Archív is not affiliated with or endorsed by the LEGO Group. We are responsible for our own data practices, not LEGO's.

1. Information we collect

Without an account: Visitors can browse the catalog without signing in. Web hosting, requests for images or catalogs, and external sites may receive technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request details. Archív does not require your name or email to browse.

With a Google account: Firebase Authentication gives Archív your unique account identifier, name, email address, and available profile photo. We do not receive your Google password. We store your owned and wishlisted LEGO set identifiers and related details, including selection timestamps, in Cloud Firestore.

Account and usage reporting: Archív also stores account profile details and first/recent sign-in dates, counts of owned and wishlisted sets, owned themes, and activity events such as an owned/wishlist change (set number, theme, action, date and time). For signed-in users, clicks on eBay shopping links may be recorded with the account identifier and time. These events are not proof of a purchase. If you choose to opt in to occasional Archív news and product updates, we store that choice as an account-associated boolean; otherwise the preference is treated as off.

Local browser or device storage: Firebase sign-in uses device or browser storage to keep you authenticated. For set inventories you choose to track, Archív saves part-checklist selections in local browser or app storage, not in Firebase. This checklist is separate from cloud-saved Owned and Wishlist selections, does not synchronize across devices, and can be erased when local site/app data is cleared.

Uploaded Photographs and Community Review

When signed-in collectors upload LEGO set photos, Archív converts them into smaller JPEGs in the browser and strips embedded source camera metadata such as GPS/EXIF. Cloud Firestore stores the photo, set identifier, associated account ID, status, timestamps, and, when you voluntarily submit the photo for community review, the date and version of your display permission. Private photos are accessible to their account owner. Authorized Archív moderators can view pending submissions to decide whether to approve or reject them.

If approved, a separate photo copy may appear in the public community catalog, without the contributor's name, email address, or account ID in the public record. Contributors retain photo copyright. Approved photos may remain in the catalog after the contributor deletes their account, but photographers and authorized rights holders can request removal through the photo's Request removal link or getshelfiecollect@gmail.com. We may ask for reasonable proof of authorship or authorization before processing removal requests.

Deleting an Archív account removes its private, pending, and rejected photo records, plus the account-linked working copies of approved photos. It does not automatically remove separately approved public community photos. We may retain limited, nonpublic records of the community display permission, including an account identifier, consent time, and permission version, for an appropriate period to help resolve copyright and removal requests. Only authorized administrators can access these permission records. See our Community Photo Terms.

2. How we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, save and synchronize owned and wishlisted sets, display collection status, operate and secure the service, provide support, and maintain private activity and collection reporting. Your saved collection and activity are not published as a public profile.

Authorized Archív administrators can access account profiles, collection summaries, and activity reports to operate the service. Other collectors do not receive access to your private records. Hosting and cloud-service providers may process technical and account information to provide the service.

3. Hosting, images and third parties

GitHub Pages hosts the public website. Google Firebase provides authentication and Cloud Firestore storage. LEGO set information is drawn from Rebrickable datasets, and product images and source references may be loaded from Rebrickable or other image hosts. These hosts can receive technical request information when content loads.

Archív may link to eBay through the eBay Partner Network and receive a commission if a qualifying purchase occurs. When you visit eBay or another third-party site, that site may set cookies, process your information, and apply its own terms and privacy policy. Archív does not receive your payment card information or verify your marketplace purchases.

4. Cookies, analytics and affiliate tracking

Archív uses Firebase authentication storage and local checklist storage as described above. When signed in, clicking an eBay shopping link can create an account-associated click event in Cloud Firestore for private administrative reporting. It records link interactions, not purchases, and guest clicks are not stored in this reporting feature. The current published Archív website does not intentionally load a standalone Impact advertising/affiliate analytics script. It does not use Firebase Analytics. Third-party destinations and embedded external images may independently use cookies or similar technologies subject to their own policies. You can control cookies and local storage through your browser and device settings, but clearing sign-in storage may log you out.

5. Account and data retention

We generally retain your account information, collection selections, communications preference, and associated activity while your account is active. We do not currently apply a separate automatic expiration period to these records. You can manage your communications preference in My profile after signing in. This optional setting is off unless you actively enable it. It records whether you would like to receive occasional Archív news and product updates; the preference does not itself send email, and Archív does not currently operate a marketing email system.

Delete your Archív account yourself: On the Archív website, sign in and open My profile, choose Delete profile, type DELETE, and confirm your identity by signing in with Google again. Deletion removes your Archív Firebase Authentication account, cloud-saved Owned and Wishlist records, groups if any, account profile and communications preference, collection summaries, and recorded account activity, including associated eBay link clicks. It also deletes personal photo records and account-linked photo copies. Separately approved community photos may remain unless removed by request or moderation, as described in Uploaded Photographs and Community Review. This action cannot be undone. It does not delete your Google account or eBay account.

To help prevent an old signed-in session from recreating deleted data, Archív retains a minimal deletion marker containing a Firebase account identifier and a deletion flag, without your name, email address, or collection contents. Checklist selections saved locally on the device where you delete your account are cleared, but local checklist data on other devices is not synchronized and must be cleared through those devices' browser or app storage.

If a deletion is interrupted, some account information may have been removed already. You can retry the deletion process while the account still exists or contact us for help. Infrastructure providers may retain operational logs or backups under their policies, and certain records may be retained when legally required.

For access, correction, export, or deletion assistance, email getshelfiecollect@gmail.com. Archív does not currently offer a self-service data export in this LEGO interface. We may need to verify that a request comes from the account owner before acting on personal information.

Deleting Archív records does not delete your Google account or third-party eBay account. Providers may retain certain technical logs or backups under their own policies or where retention is legally required. Please contact us if you need help exercising privacy rights applicable to your location.

6. Children

Archív is a general collection-management service and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to create accounts for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 provided personal information, contact us so we can review and address the information.

7. Security and changes

We use Firebase authentication and database access controls to restrict access to account collections and reporting. No internet-based service can guarantee perfect security. We may update this policy if the app, service providers, or information practices change, and will revise the updated date when we do.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact getshelfiecollect@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or verification codes. For use of the Service, see our Terms of Use and Contact page.
